Start with low dose

As dose increases, both groups depress CNS activity through GABA-A facilitation. The important teaching point is what happens at higher doses.

Benzodiazepines Flatter curve: effect plateaus because they need endogenous GABA and mainly increase channel opening frequency.
Barbiturates Steeper curve: CNS depression keeps rising; at high doses they can directly activate GABA-A channels.
Exam line Barbiturates have no protective ceiling, so dose escalation can progress to fatal respiratory and CNS depression.
Teaching script: “Both enhance GABA-A inhibition, but benzodiazepines show a ceiling effect when used alone. Barbiturates continue down the CNS depression pathway: sedation, hypnosis, anesthesia, coma, death.”
