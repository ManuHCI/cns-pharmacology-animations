GABA alone

GABA binds the GABA-A receptor and opens the ligand-gated Cl- channel. Chloride enters the postsynaptic neuron, hyperpolarizing it and decreasing excitability.

Core receptor GABA-A is an ionotropic receptor: activation opens a central chloride channel.
Physiologic effect Cl- influx makes the neuron less likely to fire an action potential.
Exam hook Think: inhibitory neurotransmission, sedation, anxiolysis, hypnosis, anticonvulsant effects.

High-yield Comparison

Feature Benzodiazepines Barbiturates
Main action at GABA-A Positive allosteric modulators; require GABA for channel opening. Positive allosteric modulators; enhance GABA effect and at high dose can directly activate the channel.
Channel effect Increase frequency of Cl- channel opening. Increase duration of Cl- channel opening.
Safety teaching point Ceiling effect when used alone; overdose is much more dangerous with alcohol, opioids, or other CNS depressants. Narrower therapeutic index; greater risk of profound CNS and respiratory depression.
Antagonist hook Flumazenil reverses benzodiazepine effects in selected cases. No equivalent routine antagonist; supportive care is central in toxicity.
