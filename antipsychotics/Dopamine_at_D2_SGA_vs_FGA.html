Dopamine vs Antipsychotic at the D2 Receptor SGA vs FGA

· Dept. of Pharmacology, MAMC · 2nd Year MBBS / MD
① SGA bound to D2 — dopamine far away
Speed: 1.0×
Outcome at D2 Receptor
🔵 SGA (Atypical) Fast Koff → drug spontaneously dissociates → DA fills the empty pocket. → No EPS · No ↑Prolactin
🔴 FGA (Typical) Very slow Koff → drug stays bound → DA cannot displace it, bounces off. → EPS + ↑Prolactin
Pharmacology note: FGAs are not literally covalent — but Koff is so slow (haloperidol ≈ 30 min on D2 vs clozapine < 1 min) that it's functionally non-displaceable during a DA surge.
Drugs to Remember
  • FGA: Haloperidol, Fluphenazine, Chlorpromazine, Trifluoperazine
  • SGA: Clozapine, Quetiapine, Olanzapine, Risperidone, Aripiprazole
  • Tip: Among SGAs, Risperidone has the slowest Koff → most prolactin elevation in this group.
